## Authentication, authorization and tenant isolation

- Backend grants are derived from current database membership and requested tenant. Owner and Manager have finance and reporting grants, only Owner manages members, Staff can prepare and enter orders and collect permitted counter payments, and Driver uses assignment-scoped delivery access. Staff delivery access is also assignment-scoped. There is no implemented `ACCOUNTANT` role. Platform Admin authorization uses the server UUID allowlist and is separate from restaurant ownership. [Role matrix](role-permission-matrix.md)
- Customer identity is the authenticated subject. Saved addresses and profile are account-wide. M12 orders, inbox, device registrations and checkout receipts also carry the restaurant scope. A signed-in customer may intentionally order from another restaurant through its public catalog with that restaurant’s valid inputs. An Android package, URI or selected UUID confers no access to another user’s records or restaurant staff routes.
- The review traced validated UUID and enum inputs, parameter-bound JDBC/JPA operations, method permissions, scoped resource lookups, multipart limits, private receipt/avatar handling, and notification enqueue and dispatch checks. Signed avatar URLs expire. Public restaurant logos and menu images are served through separate buckets, with writes through Spring. Repository code and migrations define the expected boundaries. Deployed Storage access, privilege grants, CORS/ingress and provider behavior require separate environment evidence.
- Guest QR credentials are checked in Spring and sent outside URL query strings by the normal client. A valid Common QR capability is scoped through the Guest proxy to the resolved tenant. Legacy token-only queue routes remain capability-based. Rotated/disabled QR and wrong credentials are denied. QR/queue expiry and general per-route abuse throttling are absent. Size limits and token checks provide different protections from rate limiting.
- The customer FCM dispatcher sends generic delivery payloads. Private detail remains in the authenticated inbox. Mobile logout can queue native revocation for reconnect and gate a new registration on that cleanup. Provider token revocation and delayed delivery on shared hardware are blocked. ## Delivery, inventory and cash reconciliation

- The real PostgreSQL checkout test follows a customer’s confirmed destination, authoritative prices, stock, order and pending COD payment into staff preparation, assignment, reassignment, pickup and confirmed cash completion. History follows the customer and rejects a different account. Owner sales, cashbook and close reconcile the resulting collection. The database fixture verifies the immutable order coordinates and saved-address reference after an address is edited or removed.
- Issued-key tests exercise shared quotes, delayed POST before/after explicit abandonment, same-key exact replay, changed body and cross-scope keys. Client tests exercise timeout, receipt lookup, conflicting attempt recovery, pending-key restart state and rapid taps. A single committed attempt has one order, one payment and one stock deduction.
- Pending and Accepted unpaid customer delivery cancellation releases proven stock. Preparing retains stock for possible consumption. Paid COD remains a recorded collection and requires separate refund reconciliation. Ready, Out for Delivery and terminal transitions follow state guards. Historical `NULL` reservation evidence remains unknown. Cancellation records the actor and reason, and a forced downstream write failure rolls back the order, payment, reservation and history together.
- Cashbook uses confirmed collection and payout records. Sales cohort totals, original collections and dated refunds/reversals have separate sources. Purchase obligations remain separate from payment allocation, expense recognition and stock valuation. Local tests cover purchase and payable history, payment allocation and reversals, private receipts, posted records, exact integer money, local-day/month boundaries, duplicate collection and closing controls. ## Reliability and remaining coverage

- The final loopback probe launched 30 concurrent keyed Common QR requests across 10 restaurants using the default five-connection Spring pool. It measured **411 ms** for the HTTP batch. It produced 20 orders and 10 exact replays, allocated queue numbers 1 and 2 per restaurant and deducted two units per tenant. An earlier run measured 813 ms. This is a bounded fixture and provides no production latency target.
- Order and history queries cap requested page sizes. Public menu fetches variants and modifiers in batches. Delivery list mapping performs an order lookup for each page row. The Customer Home, Menu and Product screens can request the full menu separately. Large menu rendering, production-size histories and long-lived mobile resume have no measured profile in this run. The advisory accounting lock and row locks serialize overlapping writes within a tenant. Separate tenant keys allow independent workloads.
- Spring emitted a compatibility warning for direct `PageImpl` JSON serialization. The tested clients received the expected page shape. The warning is a consideration for future Spring upgrades.
- Staff counter ordering protects rapid taps and asks the operator to confirm uncertain orders before retrying. Its guard is in memory and the endpoint lacks durable replay. Legacy direct saved-address checkout and reorder endpoints also lack the issued-key guarantee. The current Customer app uses the M12 path and rebuilds reorders as a fresh cart. A delayed request after restart or manual override can still duplicate a staff or legacy order.
- The product balance and immutable delivery reservation evidence support checkout and early delivery cancellation. The established counter/QR cancellation workflow retains a manual stock review and does not have historical per-order reservation evidence. The appropriate counter/QR stock disposition, and cancellation after an explicitly refunded delivery, need developer policy before changing these behaviors. Posted financial records and their correction history remain authoritative.
- Product purchase, quantity on hand, expenses, net sales and COD cash custody have separate contracts. The repository has no complete inventory movement ledger, full double-entry ledger, cost-of-goods valuation, online payment/refund provider or dedicated bank reconciliation feature. These are coverage boundaries.
