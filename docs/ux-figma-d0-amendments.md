## Shared amendment acceptance

These requirements apply to every linked row; they specify future design acceptance, not implementation in D0.

- **M — modifiers:** Keep variants and configured required/optional modifier groups/options. Show backend selection limits, availability and prices. Missing required choices or invalid min/max selection blocks Add and has an associated error. Notes do not satisfy required choices. Summaries show selected modifiers; actual pricing, stock and immutable order snapshots remain server-authoritative. A synthetic design example must be labelled as an example, not a real catalog assertion.
- **E — editorial:** Use **Featured / Restaurant pick**, curated by Owner for a stable same-tenant product ID. Mala is the approved initial intent only after its real UUID is verified. No calculated popularity, category-order/name/position inference, or Manager editorial grant. Unavailable/absent designation must have an honest fallback. The persistence/public contract is future UXI-05 work.
- **T — theme:** Backend runtime profile/logo/palette authority; Owner/Manager palette edits, Owner-only logo upload. Safe same-tenant refresh and failed-save/failed-refresh/stale-version states must be designed. Native launcher icon/splash/name, package ID, tenant binding, origins/signing/deep links remain reviewed release metadata. San Thaw Tar red is one tenant's palette, with contrasting tenant examples and distinct danger semantics.
- **D — evidence and deferrals:** Tiny Figma fills are previews. Full-resolution approved originals, integrity/quality/crop approval and exact restaurant/product/variant UUID mapping remain pending. No reconstructed originals or live uploads. Deferred persistence/provider/report extras must be marked design-only/deferred, not working controls.
- **L — lifetime:** Bound cart/form retention to actual current session/open-form behavior. Durable checkout-attempt identity/recovery is separate; preserve exact request body/key and original scan requirements. Do not promise durable offline drafts, restored queue tokens or automatic second orders/money postings.
- **F — finance:** Preserve exact decimal strings, order-cohort sales versus dated collections/refunds, restaurant-local Day/Month/Year/Range up to 366 days, existing daily/monthly sales trends and three private CSV families. No invented profit, cash chart series, custom schema or physical-row-count preview. Shopping plans never post purchases; immutable history and authorization remain intact.
