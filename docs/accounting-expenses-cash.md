# Operating expenses and cash reconciliation

All endpoints are under `/api/v1/restaurants/{restaurantId}/accounting`. Owner and Manager financial permissions are checked on every service entry point. Every mutation requires `Idempotency-Key`; retain the key and unchanged body when retrying. Successful retries return the same financial record. Whole-currency amounts are signed 64-bit integers in the database and decimal strings in response JSON; no binary floating-point arithmetic is used.

## Expenses

`GET/POST /expenses`, `GET /expenses/{id}`, `POST /expenses/{id}/payments`, `POST /expenses/{id}/payments/{paymentId}/reverse`, and `POST /expenses/{id}/cancel` implement the expense register. GET accepts inclusive `from`/`to`, `category`, server-side `search`, `paymentStatus`, and zero-based `page`/`size` (maximum 200). Details include chronological payment records, original payment timestamps, cancellation reasons and actors, and payment reversal timestamps, reasons and actors. Optional `receiptId` must belong to this restaurant; private receipt upload/view uses the shared accounting receipt API.

The permitted categories are `RENT`, `SALARIES_WAGES`, `ELECTRICITY`, `WATER`, `INTERNET`, `TRANSPORT`, `PACKAGING`, `EQUIPMENT_MAINTENANCE`, `MARKETING`, and `MISCELLANEOUS`. Inventory purchases belong in the purchase register; no inventory category or automatic purchase-to-expense posting exists. Users must not manually record the same supplier obligation in both registers.

Expense date is the accrual/business date. A separately posted payment has its own exact `paidAt` timestamp and `CASH`, `BANK_TRANSFER`, `CARD`, or `OTHER` method. Pending obligations are `UNPAID`; net unreversed payments below the amount produce `PARTIALLY_PAID`; equal payments produce `PAID`. Payment amounts cannot exceed the remaining balance, and cancelled expenses cannot be paid. All payment writers serialize per restaurant. Cancelling requires reversing each existing payment first. A payment reversal records an actual refund/correction received at reversal time and requires a reason; it never removes the original cash movement from its original day. If cash was not actually returned, an authorized compensating movement is required to reconcile physical cash.

`GET /expenses/report?from=...&to=...&period=DAY|MONTH|YEAR` returns gross posted amounts, cancelled amounts and current net expense amounts by category and date bucket. Cancellations are shown against the original expense period; the immutable adjustment and recording timestamps remain available in detail and audit history. Daily ranges, complete months, and complete years use the same endpoint (maximum 366 calendar days).

`GET/POST /expense-templates` stores immutable `MONTHLY`/`YEARLY` recurring templates. Templates do not create expenses automatically. `POST /expense-templates/{id}/confirm` with `{ "expenseDate": "YYYY-MM-DD" }` explicitly posts one eligible occurrence. A unique restaurant/template/date constraint prevents duplicate posting, even with different request keys. Monthly dates preserve the original day anchor, clamping to month-end in shorter months; yearly February 29 occurrences clamp to February 28 in non-leap years. `POST /expense-templates/{id}/stop` appends a stop record with a reason; posted occurrences remain visible. Corrections cancel and replace records with an audit reason.

## Cashbook

`GET /cashbook/daily?date=...` reports restaurant-local cash only: opening balance, collected cash sales, cash refunds paid, cash purchase payments, cash expense payments, other receipts/payments, expected closing balance and the current finalized count. This is a consolidated restaurant cash account, including delivery-driver cash custody; it is not a record of till handover. Count all cash held for the restaurant, including cash with drivers. Explicit bank deposit and withdrawal movements change consolidated physical cash; handing cash from driver to cashier does not. `CASH_AT_COUNTER` and `CASH_ON_DELIVERY` count when the payment is `PAID` (or a historically `REFUNDED` original collection) and has a genuine payment timestamp. `ONLINE` and all other non-cash methods are excluded. Payment reversals are separate receipts on their recording day. Unpaid sales never become cash receipts.

Legacy cash payments without `paid_at`, or legacy `REFUNDED` payments without complete append-only refund events, appear in `missingInputs`. Their unknown collection/refund amounts and dates are never reconstructed from later updates. The expected balance includes only verifiable dated events and must be reconciled to source records, actual cash and any necessary authorized adjustment. Finalization with these gaps requires `acknowledgeMissingInputs: true` and a nonblank `reconciliationReason`; the closing preserves the reason and the missing-input snapshot, and the audit preserves the exact confirmation. Acknowledgment records the responsible manager's reconciliation; it does not invent a refund timestamp.

`GET /cashbook/transactions?from=...&to=...&page=...&size=...` returns a chronological, paginated union of the source transactions and append-only reversals. Cash refund reversals are visible as `SALE_REFUND_REVERSAL` receipts on their actual recording date and contribute to other receipts; original refund outflows remain on their original dates. Source IDs permit tracing back to sales, purchases, expenses and authorized cash movements. Source ledgers are aggregated independently, preventing payment/order/item join multiplication.

`POST /cashbook/openings` records one initial counted balance `{date,amount,reason}` for the restaurant. Every following day opens with the previous day's finalized actual count; missing intervening counts must be completed in sequence. A missing opening appears as `null`, and finalization is refused. This starting balance establishes the cashbook cutover; earlier history remains visible but does not invent an opening count.

`POST /cashbook/movements` records authorized `OTHER_RECEIPT`, `OWNER_DEPOSIT`, `OWNER_WITHDRAWAL`, `BANK_DEPOSIT`, `BANK_WITHDRAWAL`, or `ADJUSTMENT` events with `direction` (`IN`/`OUT`), positive `amount`, exact `occurredAt`, and reason. These types represent additional physical cash events, not copies of existing sales/purchase/expense transactions. `POST /cashbook/movements/{id}/reverse` appends the opposite signed amount at reversal time.

`POST /cashbook/closings` takes `{date,actualCash}` (and the explicit legacy-gap acknowledgment above where required) and atomically snapshots the expected cash and variance (`actualCash - expectedCash`), with responsible member and closing timestamp. Expected cash equals opening plus collected cash sales and other receipts, less cash refunds, cash purchase payments, cash expense payments, and other cash payments. The next opening uses actual counted cash, preserving variance visibly. Historical closings include any `reconciliationNote`; the accounting audit workspace exposes the responsible actors and reasons.

Finalized records cannot be updated or deleted. Cash writers share the same transaction lock and reject posting on or before the latest nonreversed finalized day, preserving subsequent carry-forwards. `POST /cashbook/closings/{id}/reverse` requires a reason and only permits newest-first reversals. Reclosing creates a new revision without overwriting the original; `GET /cashbook/closings` includes all originals, revisions and reversal metadata. Reversing a close itself does not move cash.

Apply forward-only migrations through the normal reviewed database process. No production migration is automatically applied. RLS and revoked client privileges keep writes in the Spring API; Hibernate remains configured for schema validation.
